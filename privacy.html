Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Unsolved Files Publisher is an internal content-publishing tool used solely by the Unsolved Files studio to upload its own videos to social platforms (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok) via their official APIs.

What we collect

Nothing from the public. The application does not have end users, does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of third parties, and does not use cookies or tracking.

Platform access

The application accesses only the studio's own social media accounts, using OAuth tokens granted by the account owner. Tokens are stored locally on the studio's private machine and are never shared with third parties. Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's security settings.

Content

Published videos are based on public court records and published press reports about historical cases.
