🔎 Unsolved Files

Real unsolved cases in 60 seconds. Unsolved Files is a short-form documentary studio publishing daily true-crime mini documentaries about historical cold cases, based on public court records and published press reports.

Our videos are produced with an in-house automated pipeline (open source) and published to YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok through their official APIs.
