Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Velzon Social Publisher is an internal content-publishing tool used solely by Velzon (velzon.tr) to upload its own educational stock-market content to social platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X) via their official APIs.

What we collect

Nothing from the public. The application does not have end users, does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of third parties, and does not use cookies or tracking.

Platform access

The application accesses only Velzon's own social media accounts, using OAuth tokens granted by the account owner. Tokens are stored on Velzon's own private server and are never shared with third parties. Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's security settings.

Content

Published content summarizes Velzon's own knowledge-base articles about stock-market indicators, terminology, and platform features — it is educational/informational, not financial advice.
